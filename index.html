NASA C-MAPSS · Jet engines

TrustRUL

Remaining useful life of jet engines. A GRU with Monte Carlo dropout, plus conformal prediction that raised interval coverage from about 79 % to about 89 %. Explained with SHAP, tracked in MLflow and packaged with Docker.

  • Conformal prediction
